Across all 11 stories, right-minus-left differences were nominally positive for full-group, participant split-half, and leave-one-participant-out analyses. None survived FDR correction across the nine scope-by-analysis paired tests (smallest q=0.136316). The development and held-out contrasts were also not significant when evaluated separately, so this result does not establish lateralization.
